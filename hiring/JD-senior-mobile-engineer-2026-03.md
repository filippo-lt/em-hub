# Senior Software Engineer — Mobile

> **Status:** Draft — March 2026
> **Replaces:** Senior iOS Developer (Tech Advisor) — Developer Advisor model discontinued
> **Context:** AI Transformation Program — platform-based, AI-native ownership model

---

## About the Role

At Leadtech, we're changing how mobile software gets built. As part of our AI Transformation Program, we're looking for a Senior Software Engineer — Mobile who will own end-to-end delivery of iOS and Android applications across two products — using AI tooling as a core part of the workflow, not an afterthought.

This is a full-ownership role. You write the code, make the architectural calls, and ship the product. There is no external team beneath you and no technical lead above you — you are the decision-maker. AI is your force multiplier; Product is your partner.

You'll work directly with Product Managers and Designers to turn ideas into working software, own both mobile platforms across your projects, and maintain the AI-assisted development environment that makes this model possible.

---

## What You'll Do

- Own end-to-end delivery of 2 mobile products: from architecture to App Store and Play Store release
- Write production-quality iOS applications in Swift and SwiftUI (your primary platform)
- Own Android delivery on your projects in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose — using AI tooling to bridge platform-specific gaps where needed
- Maintain and evolve the AI development stack: editor configuration, prompt engineering, context management, and workflow automation
- Build internal tooling and automation that improves development efficiency across the team
- Integrate AI-powered features directly into the apps where products require it
- Build and maintain lightweight backend services and integrations when projects require it
- Collaborate closely with Product Managers and Designers to define scope, surface tradeoffs, and keep delivery predictable
- Make independent architectural decisions, with Staff Engineers available as a technical sounding board

---

## What We're Looking For

**Must-have**
- 6–8 years of software engineering experience, with strong depth in native iOS development
- Production-level command of Swift, SwiftUI, and UIKit
- Solid Android fundamentals — Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Play Store delivery
- Strong architectural judgment: you design systems that scale and know when simplicity is the right call
- Actively uses AI-assisted development tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude, or equivalent) as a core part of your daily workflow — not occasional use
- Strong communication skills — you work directly with Product, push back when needed, and set realistic expectations

**Strong plus**
- Product intuition — you think about the user experience, not just the implementation
- Experience building or maintaining internal developer tooling or automation
- Familiarity with lightweight backend development (REST APIs, third-party integrations)
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines and automated testing practices

---

## What This Role Is Not

- It is not a technical leadership role with direct reports
- It is not a coordination role managing external development teams
- It is not a role where "using AI" means occasional prompting — it means owning and continuously improving an AI-assisted development practice
