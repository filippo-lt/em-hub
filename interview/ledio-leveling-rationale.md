# Leveling Rationale — Ledio

**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Decision:** L8 — Senior Specialist
**Question evaluated:** L8 vs L9 (both "Senior Specialist"; both 10+ years, 3-year cycle, mentorship to key ICs — these do not separate the levels)

---

## The deciding fault line

Per the company ladder, the only real separators between L8 and L9 are **decision autonomy** and **vision ownership** — not seniority, experience, or breadth of reach.

- **L8** operates at business-unit *and/or corporate* level, cooperates cross-functionally, and **advises/facilitates** decision-making for executive leadership.
- **L9** has **global** leadership, **owns and guides the company's overall vision**, and makes/course-corrects key decisions **with full independence, without waiting for superior validation**.

## Assessment against the role

| Axis | Finding | Points to |
|---|---|---|
| Decision authority | Proposes and advises; leadership makes the final call and sets the vision | **L8** |
| Reach of impact | Cross-company / multiple units | Neutral — L8 already covers corporate + cross-functional work |
| Strategy ownership | Executes and advises on direction leadership sets (not unprompted company-wide strategy) | **L8** |

## Why cross-company reach did not tip it to L9

L8 in this rubric *already* includes corporate-level, cross-functional collaboration with executive leadership. Breadth of reach alone is therefore consistent with L8. The L9 jump specifically requires the role to hand over the pen on company-wide vision and grant independent course-correction authority — which this role does not.

## Conclusion

**L8 — Senior Specialist.** All decisive axes (decision authority, strategy ownership) align at L8; reach is neutral.

## Practical flag

If Ledio is genuinely *capable* of L9 but the role simply doesn't grant that authority yet, level to the **role** (L8) and name the L9 growth path explicitly in the offer/onboarding conversation — otherwise a strong hire may feel under-leveled. The gap to close for L9 is **autonomy + ownership of company-wide vision**, not reach.
