## Message to candidate

**Subject:** Preparing for your technical interview

Hi [Candidate],

Looking forward to our technical interview on [date]. To help you get the most out of the session, here's what to expect and how to prepare.

### Format

- **Duration:** 40 minutes of live coding, followed by a short discussion.
- **Setup:** Screenshare on your own machine. We'll work together on coding exercise

### What to bring

You should come to the interview with an AI coding tool you are already comfortable with, set up and working on your machine. We don't mandate a specific tool — we want to see you work with what you'd actually choose. Some common options:

- **Cursor** — [https://cursor.com](https://cursor.com)
- **Claude Code** — [https://claude.com/claude-code](https://claude.com/claude-code)
- **Codex / GitHub Copilot** — [https://github.com/features/copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) or [https://chatgpt.com/codex](https://chatgpt.com/codex)

If you don't have access to any of the above and would prefer not to pay for one before the interview, **Kilo Code** ([https://kilocode.ai](https://kilocode.ai)) is a free option that comes with around $20 of credits on signup — enough for the exercise.

You will also need:

- **Xcode** installed and running on a Mac (the repo is a SwiftUI project).
- A reliable internet connection for screensharing and AI tool access.
