Executive summary

GCP spend closed at €31.8k in June, an increase of €5.2k from May. Tattooist contributed virtually all of the absolute increase and now represents roughly 95% of our GCP bill. The central question this month is whether that growth is proportional to expected product usage and revenue.

Primary watchpoint

Tattooist has risen from €1.4k in January to €30.3k in June. That trajectory deserves an explicit owner, explanation and forward cost expectation.
