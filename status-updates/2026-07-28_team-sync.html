5 · Technical challenges — where the interesting problems are

Can we even verify an integration?

Martech SDK · decided this month

Reading identity back from Amplitude / RevenueCat / AppsFlyer is defeated by ingestion latency and attribution windows. We chose manual-first: a debug menu into SDK state, a QA guide in the repo, a triggerable CI lane, plus a duplication linter. Dropped the estimate from ~1 month to ~1 week — and gives a visible result instead of a research project.

One SDK, three platforms, one deadline

iOS ✓ · Android in progress · Flutter next

Most of the portfolio is Flutter, and the Flutter bridge can't be designed against a moving native surface. The roadmap freezes the public API first (10 stories), then builds. The named risk is honest: Android is a real skill gap right now, on a cross-platform deadline.

Nobody is watching the money taps

TraceCheck · rated "a big risk"

Eight third-party vendors integrated, several on prepaid credits, with no monitoring or alerting — and we don't have console access to most of them. A launch traffic spike could silently break core features. Also: no web crash/error monitoring (no Crashlytics equivalent). Datadog is the web standard; that's the fix.

The AI token cliff

Whole team · raised by Andrey

We burned ~1.5–2 months of tokens in a week, sustained only by vendor emergency resets. New models are hungrier. When the resets stop, throughput drops — hard. I'm setting that expectation upward so nobody is measured against a pace that was subsidised.
