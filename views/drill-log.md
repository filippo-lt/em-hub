## What the columns mean

- **Accepted** — items the counterpart asked for that were agreed to, however briefly. "Sure", "okay", "yeah" all count.
- **Dated** — of those, the ones where *you* said a date, a deadline or a displacement out loud. A date the counterpart supplied does not count.
- **Note sent** — the written update went out since the previous 1:1.
- **Raised first** — items introduced before the counterpart set the agenda. "One last thing" at the end does not count.
- **Banned** — occurrences of "Sure." / "Sure, whatever." / "Sounds good." / "I'm happy with this." / "All good." at moments where a name, a date or a condition belonged.
- **Note** — the *condition* of the meeting in a few words (artefact on screen or not, who set the agenda). Not a judgement.

No commentary in this file. The counts are the feedback.
