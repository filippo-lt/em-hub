## 🔴 The mechanism failure, and it is the whole meeting

On 8 Sep you established — and he confirmed — that **the shared agenda doc decides coverage**.
Items that get written get discussed, in the order written, and nothing has ever been skipped.

Today you opened with: *"I didn't have time to fill it, but I see that you have some points."*

So the single topic you had prepared was not on the doc. It arrived where unwritten items always
arrive: **inside his agenda, as a comment, at a moment he was already mid-flow.**

**Credit where it is due: it landed anyway.** He understood it immediately and articulated the
contradiction better than you did. But notice *why* it got airtime — his own org walkthrough happened
to open a door at exactly the right moment. That is luck, not mechanism. An unwritten item that lands
is still an unwritten item; next week the door may not open.

And the cost of arriving as an aside is not coverage, it is **closure**. An agenda item gets worked
until it resolves. An aside gets acknowledged and the walkthrough resumes — which is exactly what
happened, in about ninety seconds.

You had the prep doc. You had the number. The **two minutes of copying one sentence onto the shared
doc** is the difference between "he agrees there's a problem" and "we decided what to do about it.".
