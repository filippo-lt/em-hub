# 1-on-1 with Vlad — 2026-05-26

## The Meeting in One Line
Transfer the stakes on FaceAI, then lock the 2.5-week plan with checkpoints — so when it starts to slip, you both see it early.

## Priority Topics (in order)

1. **Carry-overs — read the list first.** (Reuse the May 18 mechanism: read aloud before any other topic. This is what broke the pattern; reuse it.)

2. **Stakes transfer — FaceAI is on its last chance.**
   - Plain words: "I want to tell you something directly. This 2.5-week plan isn't just a delivery commitment. If FaceAI doesn't get to a workable state in this window, the app gets killed / deprioritised / [whatever the actual consequence is]. I'm not telling you this to put pressure on you. I'm telling you because you came in with the plan and the timeline — you own this, and you should own it with the full picture."
   - Then stop. Let him respond. Do not soften.

3. **Lock the 2.5-week plan — make slippage visible early.**
   - Walk the list he brought. For each item: what does "done" look like, what's the dependency, who could block it.
   - Agree a mid-point checkpoint date (~end of next week). Not a status update — a "is this on track or are we already behind" call.
   - Agree the trigger: what does Vlad commit to flag, and when, if anything starts to slip. Don't accept "I'll let you know" — pin a specific signal.

4. **Name the level (carry-over from May 18 — pre-written).**
   - Literal sentence: "The way you've been operating since May — coming into the FaceAI product meeting with a plan and a timeline, doing org work, framing strategy — that's the operating model now, not a perception. I want you to know the level I'm working with you at."
   - Deliver after the stakes transfer, before the lock-the-plan section. It changes the frame from "I'm being audited" to "I'm being trusted with the real picture."

5. **Feedback probe (move out of Watch For, into Phase 1).**
   - Literal question: "What's something you wish I were doing differently right now?"
   - Ask after he's responded to the stakes transfer. Wait. Don't fill silence.
