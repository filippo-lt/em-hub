# Decision: M&A Re-Org Counter-Proposal

**Date:** 2026-04-01
**Status:** In progress — preparing counter-proposal for David Matteano

## Context

David Matteano proposed a re-org: Filippo takes over M&A (acquired app studios), David Catalá gets Filippo's one growth app. Filippo keeps Launches. Victor Jalencas (Staff Engineer, iOS) offered as a resource. Company announcement expected mid-April 2026.

The company is pushing non-organic growth — Christian wants to acquire studios, not just apps. David needs someone to bring order to the M&A mess (currently unorganized external studios: Turkish, Russian-speaking, varying quality).

### The Problem

The swap as proposed is unfunded on both sides:
- **Filippo giving up growth app:** Loses only revenue-generating app. Visibility concern — Andre Montenegro and David Catalá manage apps "paying the bills."
- **Filippo taking M&A:** M&A apps are Flutter. Filippo's team (Andrey, Vlad) is iOS-only. No Flutter skill set.
- **David Catalá receiving growth app:** Also lacks resources. Confirmed in bilateral conversation on 2026-04-01.
- **Victor offer:** Solves iOS technical leadership + M&A context. Does NOT solve Flutter gap.

### Filippo's Concerns (honest assessment)

1. Losing the growth app removes visibility and perceived value contribution
2. Flutter skill gap is real — team can't maintain what they'd inherit
3. Possible reading: launches aren't profitable, this is a way to justify Filippo's role by adding scope (no evidence — just a fear)
4. Phase 2 of AI Transformation (no externals by June 30) is at risk if team focus fragments

### Reframes That Shift the Calculus

1. **Launches + M&A = entire new-app pipeline.** Every app that eventually "pays the bills" passes through Filippo first. That's the growth engine, not a cost centre.
2. **AI Transformation Phase 3.** M&A is the next frontier: acquire app → strip external studio → onboard to AI-native model. Filippo is the only EM who can pitch this because Phase 2 is already in motion.
3. **Component library.** Filippo is building a reusable component dictionary. If M&A means building new apps from acquired IP, the person who owns the component system that cuts build time from 3 months to <1 month has infrastructure power.
4. **The Launches dept head explicitly asked for Filippo.** This is pull, not push.
5. **David said "I need you to help me decide."** This is partnership, not disposal.

## Counter-Proposal (Draft)

### Opening — Bilateral Resource Assessment

> "I've spoken with David Catalá. We're aligned on the direction, but we've both identified the same constraint: neither of us has the resources to absorb the other's scope as-is. So I want to talk about what makes this actually work."

### Structure

1. **Accept Victor** — frame as technical leadership for M&A, not as the Flutter solution.

2. **Flutter resource is non-negotiable.** Three options in order of preference:
   - Hire one Flutter engineer
   - Transfer one Flutter resource from David Catalá (he's losing M&A scope, can afford it)
   - Minimum viable: formal interim maintenance agreement — David Catalá's Flutter team maintains M&A apps until Filippo has capability, with a defined handover date

3. **Keep growth app** (preferred) OR price the swap:
   - Victor + Flutter resource + component library mandate
   - Transition timeline (8 weeks, not immediate)

4. **M&A as AI Transformation Phase 3:**
   - Acquire app → strip external studio dependency → AI-native delivery model
   - Component library as the delivery accelerator
   - Pitch to Christian: systematic reduction of time-to-value on acquisitions

5. **Protect Phase 2 timeline:**
   - No execution fragmentation before June 30
   - M&A strategic planning starts now, execution starts July
   - Victor can start bridging immediately

### What to Avoid

- Don't let Victor be the full answer to the resource question
- Don't accept the growth app swap without a clear return
- Don't present this as reluctance — frame everything as "here's how to make it succeed"
- Don't fragment Phase 2 (Andrey/Vlad) — that commitment is already made to David
