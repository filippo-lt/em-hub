# Software Engineer — Definition of Responsibilities

> This role is in its first cycle. The responsibilities below are what we are committing to; the assumptions below are what we are testing. Expect this document to evolve as evidence accumulates.

## Assumptions to Validate

- One AI-native Software Engineer can deliver what previously required an external development team.
- iOS-depth combined with AI-bridged Android delivers comparable Android quality (the 60/40 platform split holds).
- Maintaining the AI development stack fits within the SE scope without becoming a full-time job.
- Mobile-anchoring is a starting point, the role will generalizes as more SEs join.

## Delivery

- Owns end-to-end delivery of one or more products: from architecture to App Store and Play Store release.
- Writes production-quality code, with AI tooling, bridging platform-specific gaps where needed.
- Builds and maintains lightweight backend services and integrations when projects require it.
- Shares responsibility with Product for defining and executing software development Agile ceremonies (Refinement, Plannings, Dailies, etc.).
- Collaborates with Product Owners and Designers to define scope, surface tradeoffs, and keep delivery predictable.
- Responsible for bringing the product to life. Where Product Owners do not provide direction in a timely manner, the Software Engineer takes product decisions to keep delivery moving. The Product Owner remains accountable for the product itself.
- Shares responsibility and commitment with Product for defining and validating delivery dates based on scope and capacity.
- Shares responsibility with Product for ensuring that the work focuses on aligned business priorities and for on-time delivery.
- Commitment to technical and product excellence.

## Quality and Engineering Processes

- Makes independent architectural decisions on their projects, with Staff Engineers available as technical partners.
- Responsible for integrating and enforcing established internal best practices (CI/CD, branching strategies, automated testing, etc.).
- Contributes to the evolution of internal best practices based on what they learn shipping.
- Responsible for the alignment with other Software Engineers and company processes in order to have a unified and standardized workflow.

## AI Tooling and Workflow

- Uses AI-assisted development tools as a core part of the daily workflow.
- Shares responsibility for maintaining and evolving the shared AI development stack: rules, skills, agents, and workflow automation.
- Contributes patterns and learnings back to the AI stack so that improvements compound across the team.
- Commitment to keeping AI usage measurable and visible (e.g. Foreworth signals, internal AI metrics).

## People

- No direct people-management responsibility.
- This may evolve as the team grows.

## Evolution

This document is intentionally high-level and is expected to be revised at least every quarter as:

- The role generalizes off mobile.
- The AI Transformation program advances.
- Evidence accumulates against the assumptions listed above.
