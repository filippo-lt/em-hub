How we'll work — 3 rules that keep this alive

01
Extract on the rule of three

Don't promote something to the catalog until we've genuinely needed it in 3 apps. Two is a coincidence; three is a pattern. We harvest proven code — never abstract ahead of need.

02
The golden path is the easy path

If using a kit is more annoying than rolling your own, people will roll their own — and reuse dies. Every block must be the fastest way to do the thing, or it doesn't ship.

03
Everything has an owner

Each block is a maintenance commitment, not a one-off. If nobody owns it, it rots in a quarter. Ownership and a changelog are part of "done", not optional.

What this means for you

The ask for the team

  • Use them first. Reach for the catalog before writing from scratch — and tell us loudly when something's missing or painful.
  • Spot the patterns. When you copy-paste from another app for the 3rd time, that's a candidate. Flag it.
  • Own what you extract. If you pull a block out, you own its first version and its docs. Small surface, clear contract.
  • Start low-risk. We begin with scaffolding and AI skills (cheap, throwaway-friendly) before the harder versioned kits.
